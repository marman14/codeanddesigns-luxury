Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2025. AR Webcrafts LLC is committed to safeguarding your privacy and upholding rigorous data protection standards across all our digital touchpoints and client engagements.

1. Information We Collect

At AR Webcrafts LLC ("we", "our", or "us"), we collect information necessary to deliver premier custom WordPress engineering, plugin architecture, WooCommerce development, and technical consulting services. The types of data we collect include:

  • Direct Inquiries: When you submit our discovery form (#fluentform_7), newsletter form (#fluentform_2), or book an appointment via our integrated Calendly widget, we collect your full name, business email address, company URL, phone number, and project scope details.
  • Technical Engagement Data: During development engagements, we may process staging credentials, API keys, repository access, and database dumps strictly under non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) for testing and deployment.
  • Automated Data: Standard server logs, IP addresses, browser types, and anonymous behavioral telemetry to optimize our site speed, 3D WebGL performance, and user navigation.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is utilized solely to provide high-touch engineering solutions and direct technical communications. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data to any third party. Typical uses include:

  • Reviewing technical requirements and scheduling 30-minute discovery consultations with Arman and senior developers.
  • Generating bespoke architectural proposals, cost estimates, and sprint deliverables within 48 hours.
  • Executing development contracts, code reviews, and deploying tested solutions to client staging or production servers.
  • Sending periodic technical advisories, security updates, or relevant industry insights (from which you can unsubscribe at any time).

3. Data Security & Code Protection

We employ bank-grade security and developer best practices to protect your data and intellectual property:

  • All development occurs within encrypted, private GitHub/GitLab repositories with multi-factor authentication (MFA) enforced across all engineer accounts.
  • Client credentials and API secrets are never committed to public repositories and are stored in isolated encrypted secret managers.
  • Website transmissions are encrypted using standard TLS 1.3 protocols.

4. Third-Party Services

We utilize carefully selected third-party service providers to power specific website features:

  • Calendly: Embedded in our discovery call section (#appointmant) for appointment scheduling. Subject to Calendly's Privacy Notice.
  • Hosting & CDN: Deployed on high-performance global edge networks (Vercel) for rapid global asset delivery and zero downtime.

5. Your Legal Rights (GDPR & US Regulations)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have statutory rights concerning your personal data, including:

  • The right to request access to the data we hold regarding your business or inquiry.
  • The right to request immediate correction or deletion of your personal records.
  • The right to withdraw consent for marketing or newsletter communications at any time.
