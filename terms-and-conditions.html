Terms & Conditions

Standard Master Services Agreement governing software development, plugin engineering, retainer services, and technical consulting provided by AR Webcrafts LLC.

1. Engagement & Architectural Scoping

Every software development engagement with AR Webcrafts LLC begins with an architectural evaluation and discovery session. Before initiating custom development, a detailed technical specification document and milestone roadmap are agreed upon by both parties, outlining feature deliverables, sprint timelines, database requirements, and third-party integrations.

2. Development Standards & QA Process

All code produced by AR Webcrafts LLC strictly adheres to:

  • WordPress Coding Standards (WPCS): Object-oriented architecture (OOP), proper hook and filter implementation, and database query optimization.
  • Staging-First Deployment: Features are developed and verified in isolated staging environments prior to production merges.
  • Security Best Practices: Strict data sanitization, nonce verification, parameterized queries, and capability checks.

3. Intellectual Property Rights & Code Ownership

Upon receipt of final milestone payments or settlement of active retainer invoices, all custom software, custom plugins, database schemas, and tailored stylesheets developed specifically for the client become the sole and exclusive intellectual property of the client. AR Webcrafts LLC retains no proprietary lock-in on custom code produced for clients.

4. Milestone Payments & Retainer Invoicing

Projects are structured with clear milestone payments or monthly retainers. Invoices are issued electronically with standard net-terms specified in individual proposals. Work on subsequent milestones proceeds upon confirmation of milestone sign-off and scheduled payment.

5. Post-Launch Warranty & SLA Maintenance

All custom development deliverables include a 30-day post-launch warranty period covering bug fixes and adjustments related to agreed specifications. Clients enrolled in our ongoing Maintenance Retainers receive continuous security monitoring, proactive core/plugin compatibility updates, regular offsite backups, and emergency priority support.

6. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

AR Webcrafts LLC maintains strict confidentiality regarding client business logic, proprietary data, API keys, credentials, and business workflows. We readily execute mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) prior to architectural audits or code handovers.

7. Governing Law

These Terms and any project contracts executed under them shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the United States.
