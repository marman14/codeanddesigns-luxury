About AR Webcrafts LLC

Engineering Rigor.
Direct Senior Access.

AR Webcrafts LLC is a US-registered software engineering agency founded by Arman in 2019, specializing in custom WordPress plugin development, WooCommerce optimization, LearnDash LMS systems, and enterprise API integrations for growing businesses ($250K–$5M revenue).

Founder & Lead Developer • Our Journey

Muhammad Arman

Founder & Lead Developer • Principal Software Architect

“I started AR WebCrafts in 2018. I kept seeing the same problem: businesses paying $500–$800 a month for plugins that conflicted with each other, slowed their sites down, and still didn’t do what they actually needed. The obvious answer was custom development, but most agencies either charged enterprise rates or had five layers between the client and the person writing the code.”

“We built AR WebCrafts differently. Small team, all senior-level, direct communication. You talk to the developers building your project. We registered the company in the US, kept the team in Pakistan, and used the cost difference to charge honest rates without cutting corners on quality.”

“Eight years later, we’ve shipped 30+ commercial plugins, maintained a commercial webinar platform independently for 25+ months, and built everything from custom WooCommerce checkout flows to fully bespoke WordPress themes for agency clients in the USA, Germany, the UK, and Australia.”

— Muhammad Arman, Founder & Lead Developer

Our Philosophy

What We Do vs. What We Don't Do

✓ What We Do

  • Direct collaboration with senior engineers from day one
  • Custom Object-Oriented (OOP) code adhering strictly to WPCS
  • Direct database table design with indexed queries for raw speed
  • Thorough code reviews, automated unit tests, and Git versioning
  • Full code ownership with documented architectures and zero lock-in
  • Transparent timeline estimates and realistic technical assessments

✕ What We Don't Do

  • Pass your project off to junior freelancers or outsourced third parties
  • Stack 40+ bloated plugins that slow your site and create security holes
  • Bury business logic inside theme templates or fragile visual builders
  • Overpromise impossible deadlines or provide vague estimates
  • Hold your source code or credentials hostage
  • Disappear after product launch without support
